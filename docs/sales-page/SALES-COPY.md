## Tone & Voice Guidelines

### Voice Characteristics
- **Clear:** Avoid jargon when explaining value
- **Confident:** We know our product works
- **Professional:** Appropriate for security audience
- **Direct:** Get to the point

### Do's
- Use "you" and "your" to address the reader
- Focus on benefits over features
- Use numbers and specifics when possible
- Acknowledge the reader's expertise

### Don'ts
- Don't use hype words ("revolutionary", "game-changing")
- Don't oversell or make unrealistic promises
- Don't talk down to the audience
- Don't use too many exclamation points

### Example Transformations

**Bad:** "Our amazing revolutionary tool will totally change how you learn!"

**Good:** "Stop memorizing techniques. Start understanding them."

---

**Bad:** "Features include: cards, filters, history, and more!"

**Good:** "Filter by difficulty, learning path, and MITRE tactic. Track your progress with history and favorites."
